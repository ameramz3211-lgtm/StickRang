# ASO plan: StickRang vs Sticker.ly

> Final copy-paste text is in `PLAY-STORE-LISTING.md`. This file is the keyword and ranking strategy.

**Title:** `StickRang: Sticker Maker` · **Category:** Communication · **Audience:** global + desi (Pakistan, India, Gulf, UK)

## Why users will switch from Sticker.ly
Sticker.ly reviews keep repeating the same three complaints: ads everywhere, a watermark on free stickers, and needing an account. StickRang has none of these, and the background remover runs on the phone. Put that promise in screenshots and the description body (not in the title or short description, which Play doesn't allow).

## What moves organic ranking
1. **Keep rate.** No ads means fewer uninstalls, which is the biggest edge over Sticker.ly.
2. **Rating ≥ 4.5.** Ask for a review (Play In-App Review API) only after a pack is added successfully.
3. **Fresh content.** Add a pack about 3 weeks before each season: Valentine's, Ramadan, Eid, 14 August, Halloween, Christmas, New Year.
4. **Localized listings.** English (US, UK, IN) plus Urdu (ur), Arabic (ar), Hindi (hi), Indonesian (id) and Portuguese (pt-BR) translated descriptions. Sticker apps grow fastest in Brazil, Indonesia, India and the Gulf.
5. **Screenshots:** the first two sell the maker (Create, Cut-out). Sticker.ly leads with feeds; we lead with "make it in seconds".

## Play policy watch-outs
- No WhatsApp name or logo in the icon, title or screenshots. "WAStickerApps" is the accepted term; the description carries the not-affiliated disclaimer.
- No "free", "no ads", "best", "#1" or emoji in the title or short description.
- Only original sticker art. Never add celebrities, cartoon characters, film stills, brand logos or other people's memes to bundled packs.
