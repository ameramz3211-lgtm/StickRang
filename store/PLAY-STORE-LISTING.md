## Policy checklist (why this listing is safe)

- **No "WhatsApp" in the app name, icon or screenshots.** Using another company's brand there is the most common rejection for sticker apps (Impersonation / Intellectual Property policy). "WAStickerApps" is the term used by WhatsApp's own sticker developer program. The description only mentions WhatsApp to explain compatibility, with a disclaimer.
- **No "Free", "Best", "#1", "Top" or emojis in the app name or short description.** Play's Metadata policy rejects these.
- **No keyword stuffing.** Keywords appear naturally inside sentences, not as a list.
- **Original artwork only.** Every sticker was drawn for this app by `tools/make_packs.py`. No cartoon characters, celebrities, film stills, logos or memes from other creators. Keep it this way when adding new packs: never use pictures of famous people, TV/film screenshots or brand logos.
- **Fonts and emoji art are open licence** (SIL Open Font License 1.1: Lilita One, Noto Nastaliq Urdu, Noto Color Emoji). Their licence files ship inside the app at `assets/licenses/`, which the OFL requires.
- **Religious content** is respectful greetings only. Do not add Quran verses as stickers (many users and reviewers consider this disrespectful when sent casually).
- **Sticker maker content:** users make their own stickers only on their own phone; nothing is uploaded or shared inside the app, so no user-generated content moderation is needed.
