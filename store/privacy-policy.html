Effective date: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how the StickRang app ("the app", "we") handles information. In short: the app does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and your photos never leave your phone.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up, no account, no ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Photos you choose

When you use the sticker maker, you can choose a photo, GIF or short video using the Android system photo picker. The app only receives the single file you pick. The photo is processed on your device to make a sticker and is never uploaded anywhere. The "Remove background" feature uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your device; the photo itself is not sent to Google or anyone else. The finished sticker is saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

3. Sharing packs with friends

If you tap "Share pack", the app creates a file with that pack's stickers and opens Android's share menu. The file goes only where you choose to send it. If you open a pack file someone sent you, the app checks it and saves the stickers on your device. We never receive these files.

3b. Sharing stickers with WhatsApp

When you tap "Add to WhatsApp", the sticker images and pack name are shared with the WhatsApp app installed on your device, so it can show them in your chats. This happens on your device only. Once added, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to how WhatsApp handles those stickers.

4. Data retention and deletion

Stickers you make stay on your device until you delete them (long-press a sticker in your pack) or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Third parties

The background removal feature is provided by Google ML Kit through Google Play services. To deliver and improve it, ML Kit may send Google limited technical diagnostics (device model, OS version, app package and version, performance and error codes). It does not include your photos or any personal content. See Google ML Kit terms and the Google Privacy Policy. The app contains no other third-party services. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads or analytics), we will update this policy and the effective date before that version is released.
